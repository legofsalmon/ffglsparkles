Sparkles for Resolume

A source plugin that generates fireworks, sparkles, confetti, fountains and glitter over a transparent background, to overlay on your other layers or use on its own.

It installs into your Documents folder, so no administrator password is needed and nothing is changed system-wide.

Quit Resolume before continuing. Resolume scans for plugins only when it starts, so it will not see Sparkles until it is relaunched.
